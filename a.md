

2. 🧬 Majorana Fermion Lab (Kitaev Chain)
Step into the world of Topological Superconductivity, the holy grail of modern quantum computing.

The Visualization: A "Kitaev Chain" where electrons split into two Majorana Zero Modes.
The Big Concept: Demonstrate Non-Abelian Statistics by "braiding" two Majoranas around each other.
Interactive: A mini-game where the user must braid Majoranas to perform a topological quantum logic gate without decoherence.
